# Code of conduct

Aria is built by a small team and used by people who just want their phone to
work. Everyone who takes part here, in issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, is expected to keep it that way.

## What we expect

- Be direct and be kind. Disagree with the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most confusing reports come from a confusing PBX.
- Give credit, take responsibility, and say when you don't know.
- Respect people's time: search first, include the trace, keep it on topic.

## What we don't accept

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language,
publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption of a
discussion. This applies in every project space and whenever you represent the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules and
may block repeat offenders. If something happens to you or you see it happen
to someone else, report the content through GitHub or write to the maintainers
at the contact address on [5060solutions.com](https://5060solutions.com).
Reports are handled privately.
